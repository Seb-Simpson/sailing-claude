How to Sail

The wind is your engine

You cannot sail straight into the wind. The red wedge on the wind dial is the no-go zone: point your bow into it and the sails shiver and stall. Bear away until they fill.

To travel upwind, tack: zig-zag across the wind, about 45° either side.

Trim the sheet

The sheet controls how far the boom swings out. Haul in (S) when sailing close to the wind; ease out (W) as the wind moves behind you. The dashed line on the dial shows the ideal angle — match it and the trim bar turns green.

Respect the weather

In a strong blow, full sail will lay the boat on her side and strain the hull. Reef (Q) before the storm arrives. The sky will warn you.

Making your way

Harbours buy and sell goods at very different prices, offer delivery contracts, and fit out your boat. Bottles and barrels drift on the open sea. Somewhere out there, five beacons are waiting.
